## Future scope and updates

Community proposals/voting and further quests remain **planned, not implemented**. GLOW grants no voting or treasury rights. Participation rules require agreement. No mainnet, USD peg, cash returns or paid infrastructure is approved.

When evidence changes, update this checklist, `PROJECT_SUMMARY.md` and the collapsible roadmap in `apps/web/src/app/features/page.tsx`. Keep README concise and link to details. Reports belong under ignored `test-results/`, without credentials. A successful build or funded faucet must never automatically mark the GLOW game, public sign-in or publication complete.
